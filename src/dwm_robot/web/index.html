Setup & ground calibration Required before auto clean

Use the drive axle centre as the origin. Forward is +Y, right is +X. Enter centimetres. Measure the drive sprocket pitch diameter, not the overall rubber track length.

Map the image to the ground

Put four tape markers on a flat surface, forming a rectangle centred on the robot centreline. Measure near/far distances from the drive axle. The rectangle must include the pass-start distance and the target's path.

Click: 1 far-left → 2 far-right → 3 near-right → 4 near-left.

Saving a new ground calibration clears the verification checkbox. Verify again with a ruler and a harmless test object.
